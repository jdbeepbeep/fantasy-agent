Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This is a personal, non-commercial project built by an individual for personal use, monitoring their own fantasy football leagues.

What information is collected

This tool reads publicly-available and personal fantasy sports data (via the owner's own ESPN Fantasy Football account) and sends SMS text message alerts to the owner's own personal phone number.

How your mobile information is used

No mobile information collected as part of this SMS messaging program will be shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messages are sent solely to the tool owner's own phone number for personal fantasy football roster alerts.

Opting out

Since this tool sends messages only to its own owner's phone, opting out is as simple as stopping the automation or replying STOP to any message.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies -- typically a few messages per week during football season, and rarely or never outside of it. Message and data rates may apply, per the recipient's own mobile carrier plan.

Contact

This is a personal project with no commercial data collection or sharing.
